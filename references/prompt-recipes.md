# Prompt recipes

Adapt these prompts to the user's subject, aspect ratio, and text state. Keep exact counts and invariants. Do not copy every optional object into every image.

## Text-free 5:2 commercial key visual

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: text-free ultra-wide commercial cover, exact 5:2
Primary request: Create a panoramic commercial key visual in a contemporary Dunhuang mineral-pigment style. Fill the entire frame with meaningful imagery and give both left and right thirds strong visual subjects.

Scene/backdrop: a cinematic cave-gallery world built from weathered black rock, illuminated mineral-painted mural panels, distant mountains, controlled architectural details, and a reflective dark stone floor.

Subjects: on the left, one large matte ceramic vessel on a sculptural black-stone pedestal with three overlapping illuminated art panels behind it. On the right, one premium unbranded cosmetic bottle and one modern smartphone showing an image-led product composition. Use one lotus cluster in the foreground.

Composition: dual-anchor panorama. Balance the large ceramic vessel on the left against the bottle and phone on the right. Keep an open reflective path through the center. Vary panel sizes and depths. A single cinnabar, malachite, and lapis ribbon system travels across the full frame and connects the layers.

Lighting: warm directional light from the illuminated mural panels, controlled rim light on products, deep readable cave shadows, and restrained gold reflections on the floor.

Palette: charcoal cave black, sand mineral white, cinnabar red, malachite green, lapis blue, and small areas of ochre gold.

Materials: weathered rock, cracked mural plaster, matte ceramic, satin glass, brushed bronze, and reflective dark stone.

Constraints: absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, QR codes, watermarks, religious figures, or empty title area. Exact 5:2 aspect ratio. Product and device geometry must be physically believable.

Avoid: generic AI-purple gradients, cyberpunk neon, money symbols, centered symmetry, equal card grids, excessive gold, tourist-souvenir styling, fantasy-game art, flying apsaras, Buddhist figures, tiny filler details.
```

## 5:2 cover with exact title

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: X/Twitter article cover, exact 5:2
Primary request: Create a premium Chinese editorial cover in a contemporary Dunhuang mineral-pigment style.

Scene/backdrop: deep charcoal cave-wall texture with subtle mineral grain and restrained gold dust.

Composition: reserve the left 44% as a calm title zone with textured near-black material. Build a layered commercial visual-production scene across the right 56% with one luminous mural panel, one unbranded product, one device, and flowing mineral-color ribbons. Keep all important elements inside safe margins.

Text (verbatim): "【TITLE】"
Typography: bold clean Chinese sans serif; maximum two lines; render the title once; highlight only "【HIGHLIGHT】" in ochre gold.

Palette: charcoal black, sand beige, cinnabar, malachite, lapis, restrained ochre gold.

Constraints: no subtitle, author name, logo, QR code, watermark, filler copy, religious figures, or extra characters. Text must be accurate and readable at thumbnail size.
```

Palette: charcoal, sand, cinnabar, malachite, lapis, limited ochre gold.

## Remove all text from an existing image

```text
Edit target: the supplied image.
Primary change: remove every visible word, letter, number, label, seal, logo, watermark, QR code, and interface microcopy.

Reconstruct each removed area with the surrounding material and lighting. Replace text-heavy phone or panel content with image-led compositions using the established color palette.

Preserve unchanged: aspect ratio, object identities, product geometry, device geometry, composition, camera angle, light direction, reflections, shadows, ribbon flow, palette, mural texture, and architectural depth.

Output must contain absolutely no readable or pseudo-readable glyphs anywhere.
```

## Fill an empty side of a panorama

```text
Edit target: the supplied ultra-wide image.
Primary change: strengthen the 【LEFT / RIGHT】 third, which currently lacks visual weight.

Add one large foreground anchor: 【CERAMIC VESSEL / PRODUCT / SCULPTURAL ARTIFACT】 on a dark-stone pedestal. Behind it, add two or three illuminated panels at varied sizes and depths. Connect them to the existing scene using the same ribbon system, light source, palette, materials, perspective, and floor reflections.

Preserve unchanged: the opposite-side subjects, overall aspect ratio, cave architecture, color balance, established light direction, and text state.

Avoid tiny decorative filler. The added side must contain a large readable subject and clear depth.
```

## Reduce ornament without losing the style

```text
Edit target: the supplied Dunhuang-inspired commercial visual.
Primary change: reduce decorative density by removing half of the small lotus, cloud, and border motifs.

Keep one continuous ribbon system, one lotus occurrence, the main mural panel, the focal product, and the established mineral palette. Expand material surfaces and depth where decoration is removed.

Preserve product geometry, camera angle, lighting, reflections, contact shadows, aspect ratio, and text state.
```

## Convert a text-led cover to full-frame artwork

```text
Edit target: the supplied cover.
Primary change: remove the title and eliminate the empty title block. Redesign the full width as one continuous panoramic scene.

Extend mural panels, cave architecture, sculptural objects, ribbons, foreground rocks, and floor reflections into the former title area. Give the newly filled side a large anchor subject. Preserve the established commercial objects and palette on the opposite side.

Constraints: no text or accidental glyphs, no blank region, exact original aspect ratio, no religious figures.
```
